❓ HOW TO PLAY

Goal: Win 2 Yuddh (best of 3). Each Yuddh is 120 seconds — if time runs out, higher Jeevan Shakti wins. Health pools are deep and normal damage is tuned low, so a real Yuddh gives both Yoddhas time to trade combos, charge Shakti to 100% and fire a Divyastra before it ends.

📱 Hold your phone horizontal: MAHAYUDDH plays in landscape, like the wide Ranbhoomi. If you hold it upright (portrait), a rotate screen asks you to turn it sideways.

⛶ Full screen: tap FULL SCREEN, press F, or just hit YUDDH! — the game jumps into full screen on the Yuddh tap (turn this off in Settings if you don't want it). Full screen hides the game's own MAHAYUDDH bar during a Yuddh and locks the arena sideways. There is no exit pill over the arena any more — to leave full screen, pause (❚❚) and tap EXIT FULL SCREEN in the pause menu, use Settings, or use your browser / phone's own fullscreen controls. If real full screen is blocked in this view, the game fills the whole page instead and tells you, rather than silently just locking scroll. The bar above the game belongs to the app / browser itself, so no web game can hide that one — for true full screen, open the game in Chrome or Safari, or on iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen and launch it from the icon.

🕹️ Joystick (bottom-left): Drag left / right to move, push up to jump, pull down to sit / crouch. Sitting ducks under punches and cuts damage — but a GRAB still catches you.

Buttons (bottom-right): PUNCH fast · KICK longer range · GRAB up close, can't be blocked, throws them back · DIVYASTRA fires your Yoddha's DIVYASTRA — only at 100% Shakti. Below 100% the DIVYASTRA button stays inactive and just shows your percentage. Hold 💥 HEAVY with Punch/Kick for slower, stronger heavy attacks. Hold 🛡️ BLOCK to cut damage. Crouch + Punch = low blow, Crouch + Kick = sweep.

Keyboard: A D move (double-tap to dash) · W jump · S sit · J punch · U heavy punch · K kick · I heavy kick · G grab · L Divyastra · B block (hold).

Combos: chain hits inside the timing window — J+J+J Triple Jab Rush, J+J+K Akhara Slam, J+K+K Cyclone Combo, K+K Twin Kicks, crouch J+J Low Rush, crouch J+K Sweep Trap. Combo damage scales down after 4 hits so infinites don't pay.

Modes: Yuddh Chakra = beat 3 Pratidwandi in a row. Mahagatha = 3 chapters with a Maha Yoddha. Astra Vidya = no timer, dummy you can set to Still / Walking / Attacking, with reset and refill. Dwandwa Yuddh = Player 2 on ←→↑↓ + , . / ;.

Shakti: Hits you land, blocks and damage taken fill your Shakti bar. There is no attack at 50% any more — at 100% DIVYASTRA READY shows, DIVYASTRA lights up gold, and one tap unleashes your Yoddha's unique Divyastra (Arjun: Arrow Storm · Bhim: Gada Cataclysm · Zara: Lightning Tempest · Kabir: Inferno Rising · Ashwatthama: Brahmastra Fury), which spends the whole meter back to 0%.

👑 Beating Ashwatthama (Maha Yoddha): He fights in three phases — IMMORTAL WARRIOR (100–65% health), CURSED WARRIOR (65–30%, faster, shields and combos) and BRAHMASTRA AWAKENED (below 30%). He stays powerful, but every big attack opens him up: after Celestial Strike, Immortal Rage, Divine Shockwave and Brahmastra Fury his forehead jewel is EXPOSED for a few seconds and your hits — heavies most of all — deal bonus damage (watch the jewel status above the arena). When his jewel flares to charge an Astra, a well-timed HEAVY attack interrupts it (ASTRA BREAKER). Heavy attacks and combos also drain his GUARD meter; break it and he cannot block. Dodge his heavy attacks with a double-tap dash or a jump for a PERFECT DODGE counter window, and if you hold 100% Shakti when Brahmastra Fury starts charging, fire your Divyastra immediately for a DIVYASTRA CLASH. First-time hints appear during the boss fight — turn them off any time in Settings.

Tips: Mix grabs in when the CPU sits or guards. Don't spam on Hard — the CPU reads patterns.
